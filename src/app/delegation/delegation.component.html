Delegation & access roles

Suspend or ban a user's platform access role (timed or permanent) and lift it, force a user local-only with a domain-lock, and grant or revoke cluster-namespace access. Every change is written to a desired-state table and reconciled asynchronously — the admin service is the source of truth, so effects apply on the next reconcile, not instantly.
